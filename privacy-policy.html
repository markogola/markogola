Privacy Policy for Best Locker

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 · Package Name: com.bestlocker.vault · Contact: markogola82@gmail.com

Best Locker is built with a strict Zero-Cloud Architecture. Your personal photos, videos, audio files, documents, notes, and passcodes are encrypted and stored locally on your device. We do not operate cloud storage servers for your vault contents and never have access to your files or passwords.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Encrypted Vault Content: Files you import into Best Locker are encrypted on-device using 256-bit AES-GCM with keys stored inside the Android Keystore hardware-backed enclave (where supported by your device).
  • Passcodes & Decoy Configuration: PINs, patterns, passwords, and decoy PINs are transformed locally using salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hashing before saving to private app preferences. Plaintext master passcodes are never uploaded or transmitted.
  • Local Security Audit Log: Failed unlock attempts and vault security events are recorded locally in your device database solely for your review and can be cleared at any time inside Security Settings.

2. Biometric Authentication

If you enable Fingerprint / Biometric unlock, Best Locker delegates authentication strictly to the Android OS BiometricPrompt API. Best Locker never reads, copies, transmits, or stores raw fingerprint or facial biometric data.

3. File Access & Permissions

Best Locker uses Android's zero-permission Storage Access Framework (SAF) document and photo pickers so you explicitly select which files to encrypt or export. Best Locker does not request broad background access to scan your entire device storage. Normal permissions declared by the app include:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used exclusively to load Google AdMob advertisements.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT: Optional hardware biometric unlock verification.
  • VIBRATE: Tactile haptic feedback on PIN keypad, pattern grid, and stealth triggers.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Best Locker displays banner and interstitial ads via Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-1911118303200880). Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect limited technical signals such as device type, IP-derived approximate location, ad interaction telemetry, crash/performance diagnostics, and the platform Advertising ID in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Review how Google uses information from apps that use AdMob at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites . You may limit personalized advertising or reset your Advertising ID at any time in your Android, Fire Tablet, or Fire TV device settings.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Best Locker stores all encrypted files locally without external servers, you remain in complete control of your data. You can permanently shred any item inside the app, export decrypted files back to device storage, or delete all local vault data by uninstalling the app.

6. Children's Privacy

Best Locker is a utility and privacy tool intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If you have questions or requests regarding privacy, contact us at markogola82@gmail.com.
